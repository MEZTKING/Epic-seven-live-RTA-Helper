Epic 7 Engine

One toolkit for Epic Seven. Pick a module to start. Your RTA and gear data stay on this device; Guild War data is shared with your guild.

⚔️ RTA

  • Reads the draft from your emulator
  • Pick, ban and postban suggestions, each with its source
  • Opponent profiles, scouting and speed checks
  • Learns from every match you play

🛡️ Gear

  • Gear score and score after reforge
  • Keep / enhance / reforge / extract / sell
  • Which hero each item suits best
  • Shop check: worth buying?

🏰 Guild War

  • Enemy defenses, attacks and who cleared what
  • Counters from your guild's history
  • Who can take which target
  • Shared live with guildmates

➕ More coming

  • New modules will appear here

Getting started

  1. Export gear.txt from the Fribbels optimizer (RexQian fork) and import it in RTA → Data or Gear. It's shared by both.
  2. For automatic drafts, open RTA → Auto next to your emulator and calibrate once.
  3. For Guild War, one person sets up sharing (10-minute guide) and sends the invite link.
